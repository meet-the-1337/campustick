Knowledge Base & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service, pricing tiers, payment proofs, and privacy guarantees.

CampusTick provides an automated, confidential pipeline to solve and verify your Machine Learning coursework on CodeChef. First, select your service scope (Complete Course, Individual Modules, or Remaining Test Cases). Enter your CodeChef credentials and Instagram handle. If requesting a custom quote/discount, attach your syllabus screenshot and enter your college email. You can pay directly or submit to the review queue. Once logged, copy your Request ID to track status in real-time. Upon payment confirmation, our solver runs and achieves 100% verified test-case completion on your account.

• Complete Course (₹250): Solves all 7 modules (268 total problems) from 0% to 100% completion. Best bundled value for students starting from scratch.

• Individual Modules (From ₹15): Pick only the specific modules you need cleared (M1: ₹15, M2: ₹20, M3: ₹30, M4: ₹75, M5: ₹15, M6: ₹70, M7: ₹75). If all modules are selected, total is ₹300.

• Remaining Test Cases (₹15–₹150): If you have already solved 90% to 99% on your own and only have a few failing edge cases, slide the slider to your current percentage. You pay a small prorated amount (₹150 at 90%, down to just ₹15 at 99%).

For direct Complete Course orders, no college email is required at all — only your Instagram and CodeChef credentials. When requesting custom quotes or discounts, our owner personally audits your syllabus screenshots. To prevent queue spam, duplicate flooding, and abuse, college email is mandatory and restricted to one active request per email address. This guarantees genuine students receive rapid review turnarounds.
Click the "Track Request & Replies" button in the top navigation bar. Enter your Request ID (e.g. ORD-B2F9A1) or your College Email ID. The modal will display your live status, the exact timestamp when you submitted, the owner's customized reply/quote, and the countdown for our 24-hour reply commitment.

• Review Inquiries: We maintain a guaranteed 24-hour reply SLA. The owner will review your syllabus and post your final quote directly on your tracking card.

• Coursework Completion: Standard Turnaround delivers complete coursework within 24 to 48 hours. Express Priority Turnaround prioritizes your account in our runner pipeline, finishing in 2 to 4 hours.

You can pay directly through Razorpay (Cards, Net Banking, Wallets) or via UPI Apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). After payment, simply paste your 12-digit UPI UTR / Transaction ID in the payment modal and optionally attach your payment screenshot. You can also submit or update payment proof anytime using the "Track Request & Replies" modal. Once submitted, your status automatically updates to ACCEPTED.
Yes, 100% strictly confidential. We maintain an uncompromising student anonymity guarantee. Your credentials are used exclusively by our automated runners to execute coursework, encrypted during transmission, never shared or published, and purged after order completion.
